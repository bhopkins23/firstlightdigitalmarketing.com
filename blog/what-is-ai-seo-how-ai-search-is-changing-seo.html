What Is AI SEO? How AI Search Is Changing SEO in 2026

Google AI Overviews, ChatGPT search, and Perplexity are reshaping how people find businesses online. Here is what you need to know and what to do about it.

By First Light Marketing | September 10, 2026 | 14 min read

If you have searched for anything on Google recently, you have probably noticed the AI-generated answer panels that now appear at the top of many search results. Google calls them AI Overviews, and they represent the most significant change to search in over a decade. Instead of just listing websites, Google is now using artificial intelligence to synthesize answers from multiple sources and present them directly in the search results.

For business owners in Bucks County and everywhere else, this shift raises an urgent question: how do you make sure your business shows up in these AI-generated results? That is what AI SEO is about, and that is what this guide covers.

What Is AI SEO?

AI SEO — sometimes called AI search optimization or generative engine optimization (GEO) — is the practice of optimizing your website and content so that AI-powered search systems cite your business when generating answers. This includes Google AI Overviews, ChatGPT's search feature, Perplexity, Microsoft Copilot, and the growing number of AI assistants that people use to find information and make purchasing decisions.

AI SEO builds on traditional search engine optimization but adds specific strategies for how AI systems select and attribute sources. It is not a replacement for regular SEO. Think of it as an additional layer — one that is becoming increasingly important as AI-generated results take up more real estate on the search results page.

How Google AI Overviews Work

When Google determines that a search query would benefit from a synthesized answer, it uses its AI model (Gemini) to pull information from multiple web pages, combine the relevant points, and display a summary at the top of the results. Below the summary, Google shows citation links to the sources it drew from.

This is fundamentally different from the traditional search experience. Instead of presenting ten blue links and letting the user decide which to click, Google is now deciding which sources to trust and presenting their information directly. For businesses, this means that being cited in an AI Overview can be more valuable than ranking number three or four in the organic results — because the AI Overview appears first and the user may never scroll past it.

What Triggers an AI Overview

AI Overviews do not appear for every search. They are most common for informational and research-oriented queries — questions like "best SEO company in Bucks County," "how to improve local search rankings," or "what is the difference between SEO and PPC." They are less common for simple navigational searches (searching for a specific website) or transactional queries with a single obvious answer.

For Bucks County businesses, AI Overviews frequently appear for local service queries that have a research component. When someone searches for "best dentist in Doylestown" or "SEO agency near Newtown PA," Google may generate an overview that lists several businesses along with details about their services, reviews, and differentiators. Being one of the businesses cited in that overview is extremely valuable.

How Google Chooses Which Sources to Cite

Google's AI does not randomly select sources. Research and observation from the SEO industry points to several consistent patterns in how AI Overviews choose which pages to cite:

  • Existing organic rankings matter. Pages that already rank on page one for a query are far more likely to be cited in an AI Overview for that query. AI SEO does not bypass traditional SEO — it builds on it.
  • Content that directly answers questions gets cited. AI systems are looking for clear, concise answers. Pages with FAQ sections, well-structured headings, and direct question-and-answer formatting are easier for AI to extract from.
  • Topical authority carries weight. Sites that demonstrate deep expertise on a topic — through multiple interlinked pages, blog posts, and comprehensive coverage — are more likely to be selected than sites with thin content on a single page.
  • E-E-A-T signals influence selection. Experience, Expertise, Authoritativeness, and Trustworthiness — Google's quality guidelines — play a significant role. Sites with named authors, real credentials, customer reviews, and established reputations are favored.
  • Structured data helps. FAQPage schema, LocalBusiness schema, and other structured data markup make it easier for AI systems to understand and extract information from your pages.

AI SEO vs. Traditional SEO: What Is Different

Traditional SEO is about ranking your pages in Google's organic results. You optimize for keywords, build backlinks, fix technical issues, and create quality content. These fundamentals have not changed — they are still essential.

AI SEO adds several priorities on top of those fundamentals:

Content Structure Becomes Critical

AI systems do not read pages the way humans do. They extract specific pieces of information based on context cues like headings, FAQ sections, and paragraph structure. A page that buries its key information in the middle of a long paragraph is harder for AI to extract from than a page that uses clear headings and directly answers common questions.

For your Bucks County business, this means structuring your service pages with clear H2 and H3 headings that match the questions your customers ask, followed by concise answers. It means adding FAQ sections to every service page. It means writing content that can be pulled into an AI-generated summary without losing its meaning.

Topical Depth Over Keyword Volume

In traditional SEO, targeting a wide range of keywords across many pages was a common strategy. AI SEO rewards depth over breadth. Instead of publishing 20 thin pages targeting slightly different keyword variations, it is more effective to create comprehensive resources that thoroughly cover a topic from multiple angles.

For example, rather than having separate shallow pages for "SEO services Newtown PA," "SEO company Doylestown," and "SEO agency Yardley," a more effective AI SEO approach would be to have one deep, authoritative page on SEO services in Bucks County, supported by blog posts that explore specific subtopics in detail. The interlinked cluster of content signals topical authority far more effectively than scattered standalone pages.

Brand Signals and Reputation

AI systems are increasingly sophisticated at evaluating brand reputation. Google Business Profile reviews, citations across the web, mentions in local publications, and consistent NAP (Name, Address, Phone) data all contribute to how AI perceives your business. A business with 50 genuine Google reviews and active social profiles will be treated differently than one with no reviews and no online presence beyond a website.

What Bucks County Businesses Need to Do Right Now

AI search is not a future trend — it is happening now. Here is a practical checklist for business owners in Newtown, Doylestown, Yardley, Langhorne, and across Bucks County who want to stay visible as search evolves.

1. Add FAQ Sections to Every Service Page

This is the single highest-impact change most businesses can make. Add a section of 5 to 8 frequently asked questions to each service page on your website. Write clear, direct answers that a customer would find genuinely useful. Then add FAQPage schema markup so search engines can easily identify and extract these Q&A pairs.

2. Claim and Optimize Your Google Business Profile

For local businesses, your Google Business Profile (GBP) is a primary data source for AI Overviews. Make sure your profile is fully completed with accurate business information, service categories, photos, business hours, and service area. Regularly post updates, respond to reviews, and keep your information current.

3. Build Topical Authority Through Content

Start a blog and publish in-depth articles about topics relevant to your industry and your local market. A plumber in Bucks County should be writing about common plumbing issues in older homes (Bucks County has plenty), seasonal maintenance tips, and how to choose a plumber. A marketing agency should be writing about SEO, PPC, and social media strategies specific to the local business landscape. Each piece of content strengthens your topical authority and gives AI systems more material to cite.

4. Implement Structured Data Markup

At minimum, your website should have LocalBusiness schema on every page (or Organization schema for non-local businesses), FAQPage schema on pages with FAQ sections, Article schema on blog posts, and BreadcrumbList schema for navigation. This structured data helps AI systems understand what your pages are about and how to categorize the information on them.

5. Strengthen Your E-E-A-T Signals

Put real names and credentials on your about page. If you have certifications, awards, or industry affiliations, display them. Publish original content that demonstrates genuine expertise. Get mentioned in local publications and community organizations. These signals tell AI systems that your business is a trustworthy source of information.

6. Monitor Your AI Search Visibility

Start paying attention to how your business appears in AI-generated results. Search for the queries that matter most to your business and note whether an AI Overview appears, whether your site is cited, and which competitors are being featured. This is the new competitive intelligence — understanding not just where you rank, but whether AI is recommending you.

Key Takeaway

AI search does not replace traditional SEO — it builds on it. Businesses that already have strong organic rankings, quality content, and solid local SEO are best positioned to be cited in AI Overviews. The businesses that start optimizing for AI search now will have a significant head start over competitors who wait.

Frequently Asked Questions About AI SEO

What is AI SEO?

AI SEO is the practice of optimizing your website and content to be cited and featured in AI-powered search results, including Google AI Overviews, ChatGPT search, Perplexity, and other AI assistants. It builds on traditional SEO but emphasizes structured content, topical authority, and E-E-A-T signals that AI systems use to select trustworthy sources.

What are Google AI Overviews?

Google AI Overviews are AI-generated answer panels that appear at the top of Google search results for many queries. Instead of listing 10 blue links, Google uses AI to synthesize an answer from multiple web sources and displays it prominently with citations. Getting cited in an AI Overview can drive significant traffic because it positions your site as a trusted authority on the topic.

How do I get my business featured in Google AI Overviews?

To get featured in Google AI Overviews, focus on building topical authority through in-depth content, use FAQ sections with structured data markup, maintain a strong Google Business Profile for local queries, and ensure your content directly answers the questions people search for. Pages that already rank on page one organically are most likely to be cited in AI Overviews.

Does AI search replace traditional SEO?

No. AI search builds on top of traditional SEO rather than replacing it. Google AI Overviews primarily cite pages that already rank well organically. The fundamentals of technical SEO, quality content, backlinks, and local optimization still matter. AI SEO adds an additional layer of optimization focused on content structure, question-answer formatting, and authority signals that AI systems prioritize.

How does AI search affect local businesses in Bucks County?

AI search is changing how local customers find businesses. When someone asks Google for the best plumber in Newtown PA, the AI Overview may recommend specific businesses based on reviews, website content, and Google Business Profile data. Local businesses that optimize for AI search can capture attention before searchers ever scroll to the traditional results, giving them a significant competitive advantage in Bucks County.

What is the difference between AI SEO and regular SEO?

Regular SEO focuses on ranking your pages in Google's traditional organic results. AI SEO additionally optimizes your content to be selected and cited by AI systems like Google AI Overviews and ChatGPT. The key differences are a greater emphasis on direct question-and-answer content, FAQ schema markup, topical depth, E-E-A-T signals, and structured data that helps AI systems extract and attribute information from your pages.
